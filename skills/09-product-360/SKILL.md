# Product 360° — Higgsfield Seedance 2.0

For pure turntable / multi-angle / hero shots of a single product. The
narrowest brief in the catalogue: one product, on a clean stage, rotating
or being rotated around. Used for Amazon listings, e-commerce hero
sections, watch / jewellery / accessories showcases.

## When to use

- "Spin / 360 / turntable / rotation."
- Watches, jewellery, sneakers, electronics, automobiles, sculptures.
- "Hero shot," "beauty shot."
- Premium / luxury product reveal.

For lifestyle / benefit-led product ads → `07-ecommerce-ad`. For broader
brand storytelling → `12-brand-story`.

## What's unique to product 360

1. **Rotation rate is everything.** 30–45°/s feels premium. 90°/s feels
   rushed. 15°/s feels luxury. Pick deliberately.
2. **Specular highlight tracking.** The whole point of a 360 is watching
   light play across the surface. Specify the highlight position and how
   it moves.
3. **Studio void or contextual stage.** Pure black / white void for hero;
   contextual stage (marble slab, fabric, water surface) for premium.
4. **Subject rotates *or* camera orbits — not both** (unless intentional).
   Rotating subject = product is the action; orbiting camera = camera is
   admiring product.
5. **No background distraction.** Subtle gradient or bokeh; no busy
   environment.
6. **Macro detail beats reveal at the climax.** End on a hero detail
   shot: dial, weave, lacing, trim.

## Product 360 hooks

| Hook                       | Phrasing |
| -------------------------- | -------- |
| **Spotlight punch**        | "Black void. At 0.5 s spotlight punches in from above; product centred and lit." |
| **Levitation entrance**    | "Product drops in from above; halts mid-air at 1 s with subtle floating bob; camera begins orbit at 2 s." |
| **Macro → wide**           | "Open extreme macro on signature detail (logo, dial, stitch). At 1.5 s rack out to full product, orbit begins." |
| **Material reveal**        | "Product begins matte / dust-covered. At 1 s a wave of light passes across, revealing finish. Orbit begins at 2 s." |
| **Studio iris-in**         | "Iris circle opens from centre over 1 s revealing product on stage." |

## Worked example — Luxury watch turntable, 8 s

```
[HOOK 0–2 s]
Black void, complete silence. At 0.4 s soft spotlight pools at centre
frame. At 0.8 s watch materialises, suspended mid-air, lit from above.
Subtle sub-bass swell. Watch is centred, dial facing camera.

[ACTION 2–7 s]
Camera orbits 270° clockwise at constant 8 ft over 5 s — 54°/s. Watch
holds position; light source remains overhead so highlights track
across:
- 2.0–3 s: lateral case profile reveals; hour markers catch light
- 3–4 s: crown side; engraving picks up specular
- 4–5 s: rear case; movement skeleton just visible through caseback
- 5–6 s: opposite lateral; bracelet articulation visible
- 6–7 s: returns to dial-front position

[CLIMAX 7–8 s]
Camera locks dial-front. Slow 5% push-in. Second hand sweeps. Brand
wordmark fades in below at 7.5 s.

[LIGHTING] Studio three-point: hard 5500 K key directly above (creating
specular tracking on case as it orbits); fill 30% from below; cool
6500 K rim back-left. Black non-reflective stage.

[CAMERA] f/2.8 throughout. Constant orbit speed. Locked focus on dial
centre.

[GRADE] Restrained teal-orange: highlights warm 30°, shadows deep cool.
Saturation 95%. No bloom.

[AUDIO] Sub-bass bed throughout. Subtle ticking foley from 4 s. Single
soft chime at 7.5 s when wordmark appears.

[OUTPUT] 8 s, 16:9 (or 1:1 for IG), 720p.
```

## Worked example — Sneaker on marble stage, 6 s

```
[HOOK 0–2 s]
White marble pedestal, soft cyclorama background. At 0.5 s sneaker
drops in from above; lands at 1 s on pedestal with a soft contact thud
and slight bounce. Settles by 1.5 s.

[ACTION 2–5 s]
Pedestal rotates clockwise at 60°/s — full revolution over 6 s, but
camera locked. Camera does not move. Subtle dolly-in by 5% across the
3 s. Specular highlight from key light tracks across mesh, swoosh, sole
as sneaker rotates.

[CLIMAX 5–6 s]
Pedestal pauses with sneaker at 45° hero angle. Macro rack-focus shifts
to lacing detail. Brand colourway tag fades in.

[LIGHTING] Three-point: 5500 K key 45° upper-left, fill 50% from below,
cool back rim 80%. Soft cyclorama backdrop.

[CAMERA] Locked frame, 50 mm equivalent. f/2.8. Subtle 5% push-in.

[GRADE] Clean and bright. Match brand palette.

[AUDIO] Soft contact thud on landing. Subtle marble-resonance reverb.
Restrained ambient pad. No score.

[OUTPUT] 6 s, 1:1, 720p.
```

## Common pitfalls

- **Rotating both subject and camera.** Reads as confused; pick one.
- **Constant 90°/s rotation.** Too fast for premium. Slow it down.
- **Too-busy backgrounds.** A 360 is a meditation on the product. Keep
  surroundings minimal.
- **No highlight tracking.** Without moving specular highlights, the
  rotation looks like a paper cutout being spun.
- **Forgetting the climax detail.** End on a macro hero shot, not just a
  return to start position.
