# Brand Story — Higgsfield Seedance 2.0

For emotional, narrative-led brand films: origin stories, mission videos,
"who we are" anthems. Distinct from product ads (`07-ecommerce-ad`,
`06-motion-design-ad`) — the goal here is feeling, not conversion.

## When to use

- Origin story, founder story, mission, vision.
- Company culture, "what we believe."
- Brand anthem video.
- 60–90 s long-form brand film (we'll generate 15 s sections).
- Pre-launch brand teasers.

For direct-conversion product ads → `06-motion-design-ad` /
`07-ecommerce-ad`. For social-platform optimised → `11-social-hook`.

## What's unique to brand story

1. **Emotion before information.** No specs, no features. The viewer
   should feel something specific (warmth, defiance, hope, focus) before
   any logo appears.
2. **Show, don't tell.** "We care about quality" is a slogan. A craftsman
   re-doing a stitch she didn't like, alone, at 2 a.m., in low light —
   that's the story.
3. **One protagonist.** Even an "us" story works through one face. Generic
   "team of people" reads as stock footage.
4. **Restraint with the logo.** The mark appears at most once, late, small.
   A logo every 2 s reads as desperation.
5. **Quietness as confidence.** The most powerful brand films have
   silence, slow pacing, and trust the viewer.
6. **VO is high-risk.** Either commit to a great script and delivery, or
   omit it. Bad VO sinks brand films faster than anything.

## Brand-story-specific hooks

| Hook                       | Phrasing |
| -------------------------- | -------- |
| **Hand at work**           | "Macro on hands working: stitching, kneading, sketching, soldering. No face yet. Craft is the protagonist." |
| **Empty space + intent**   | "Empty studio / workshop / stage. At 1.5 s a single figure walks into frame, alone." |
| **Object + meaning**       | "Macro on object that represents the brand (tool, fabric, ingredient). At 2 s rack out to context." |
| **Founder eye contact**    | "Close on founder's face, slight off-camera gaze. At 1.5 s eyes find lens; holds for a beat." |
| **Time-passage opener**    | "Time-lapse of seasons / day-cycle / construction over first 2 s. Slows to real-time at 2 s on protagonist." |

## Worked example — Founder origin, 15 s

```
[HOOK 0–2 s]
Macro on hands sketching by lamplight. Pencil scratches on paper. Warm
2700 K practical light source (visible desk lamp). Silence except for
pencil sound.

[ESTABLISH 2–5 s]
Slow pull-back: reveals empty workshop at night, single figure alone at
desk. Window behind shows snow falling. Camera handheld with subtle
1 mm jitter, 35 mm equivalent.

[ACTION 5–10 s]
At 5 s VO begins (calm, conversational): "I started this in a basement
because I couldn't find what I wanted to buy."
- 5–7 s: macro shots of work — fingers tracing a sketch, prototype on
  bench, wrench-tightening detail.
- 7–9 s: founder leans back, looks at work. Tired smile.
- 9–10 s: time cuts forward — same workshop, now full of prototypes, a
  small team working alongside.

[CLIMAX 10–13 s]
Cut to founder in present-day: standing in finished factory floor,
golden-hour light streaming through high windows. Volumetric god rays.
VO: "Six years later — still couldn't find what we wanted. So we built
it."

[RESOLUTION 13–15 s]
Wide shot: factory floor, team working. Slow camera push-in toward
finished product on bench. Brand wordmark fades in lower-right at 14 s.
Silence holds for final beat.

[LIGHTING] Practical tungsten domestic for early scenes (warm 2700 K
lamps). Volumetric god rays for present-day reveal. Always-natural
motivated sources.

[CAMERA] Handheld throughout, minimal jitter. 35 mm equivalent. Mix of
macro, medium, wide.

[GRADE] Warm nostalgia: 3200 K, lifted shadows, vintage film grain at
80% opacity. Skin tones warm.

[AUDIO] Pencil-scratching foley 0–2 s. Silence-then-piano motif arrives
at 2 s very softly (−10 dB), builds slowly. Calm VO from 5 s. Score
peaks at 13 s. Resolves to held quiet on final beat.

[OUTPUT] 15 s, 16:9, 720p.
```

## Worked example — Mission anthem, 10 s

```
[HOOK 0–2 s]
Empty stage, single overhead spotlight. Silence. At 1 s a single chair
sits in the spotlight; nobody in it.

[ACTION 2–7 s]
Camera slow push-in over 5 s. Cuts intercut between:
- 2–3 s: macro of weathered hands, gripping a tool.
- 3–4 s: wide shot of an empty road at dawn.
- 4–5 s: close-up on a person mid-thought, looking off-camera.
- 5–6 s: macro of an object being made by hand.
- 6–7 s: same chair from hook, now occupied by the protagonist of these
  cuts; she looks directly at camera.

[CLIMAX 7–10 s]
Slow push-in continues onto her face. Brand mantra appears in
typography, large and centred: a single phrase. Holds. Mantra fades.
Brand wordmark appears. Holds. Cut to black.

[STYLE] Minimal, theatrical. Single light source per shot. Restrained.

[GRADE] Cool isolation: 6500 K, slightly desaturated, restrained
contrast. Defiant, focused.

[AUDIO] Single sustained low piano note arrives at 2 s. Builds with
strings layer at 5 s. Peaks at 8 s on mantra. Held quiet at end. No VO.

[OUTPUT] 10 s, 16:9, 720p.
```

## Common pitfalls

- **Overproduction.** Gleaming, perfectly-lit "brand stories" with
  uplifting royalty-free music read as commercials, not stories.
- **Too many faces.** Pick one human anchor.
- **Logo every 3 s.** The brand earns its mark at the end. Not before.
- **Generic VO.** "We believe in quality and innovation" tells the viewer
  nothing. Either write a real script or skip VO.
- **No silence.** The most emotionally effective brand films use silence
  to let moments land.
