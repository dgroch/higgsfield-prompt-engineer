# Real Estate & Architecture — Higgsfield Seedance 2.0

For property tours, architectural showcases, interior design videos, home
staging, listing content. The brief: make a viewer imagine themselves
*inside* the space. Sell the feeling of inhabiting it.

## When to use

- Property listings (residential, commercial, luxury).
- Architectural showcase (completed projects, before/after renovation).
- Interior design firm portfolios.
- Hotel / Airbnb / vacation rental marketing.
- New-build pre-sale videos.

For pure brand storytelling about an architecture firm → `12-brand-story`.

## What's unique to real estate

1. **Continuous flow over cuts.** A single sweeping move through space
   reads premium. Multiple short cuts read as MLS. Use camera move to
   reveal, not montage.
2. **Light is the sales pitch.** Time of day, sun angle, golden hour
   spill — these *are* the property's emotional value. Specify.
3. **Negative space sells.** Empty rooms feel huge; furnished rooms feel
   defined. Decide which the property is selling.
4. **Vertical space matters.** Tilt up to ceilings, down through stairs.
   Real estate viewers care about volume, not just floor plan.
5. **Drone for context, ground for emotion.** Aerial = the location.
   Walking-pace = the inhabitation feeling. Both, in sequence.
6. **No people, or one person.** Empty rooms or a single inhabitant. A
   crowd reads as Airbnb listing. A single figure reads as aspirational.

## Real-estate-specific hooks

| Hook                       | Phrasing |
| -------------------------- | -------- |
| **Drone swoop**            | "Aerial overview at 0–1 s. Drone descends sharply at 1 s toward property; reveals facade by 2 s." |
| **Door-opening reveal**    | "Camera at exterior door threshold. Door swings open at 1 s; camera moves through into interior at 2 s." |
| **Window-to-view**         | "Macro on window glass at 0–1 s. Rack focus shifts to view beyond at 1.5 s; camera dollies through window perspective at 2 s." |
| **Detail-then-room**       | "Macro on architectural detail (carved railing, marble vein, brass fixture). Pull out to reveal room at 2 s." |
| **Light-flooded reveal**   | "Dim interior 0–1 s. Curtains pulled / lights turn on / sun rises at 1.5 s; room floods with light at 2 s." |

## Worked example — Luxury home tour, 15 s

```
[HOOK 0–2 s]
Aerial wide shot, golden hour. Modernist house perched on coastal cliff,
ocean stretching to horizon. Drone descends sharply at 1 s toward
front facade — large pivot door visible centre of facade.

[ESTABLISH 2–5 s]
At 2 s drone hand-off match-cut to ground level: camera tracks forward
through front door into vast double-height entry hall. Slow Steadicam
move at 2 ft/s. Marble floors, suspended steel staircase, golden-hour
light streaming through floor-to-ceiling windows ahead.

3.5 s: camera reaches centre of entry; tilts up to reveal volume of
ceiling. Volumetric god rays through skylight.

[ROOMS 5–11 s]
Continuous Steadicam flow through space:
- 5–7 s: glides forward into open-plan living room. Floor-to-ceiling
  windows reveal ocean view. Curtains drift in sea breeze. Camera
  pauses, slow 360° micro-orbit (45° rotation) to show full living
  space.
- 7–9 s: continues through to kitchen — slow rack-focus from foreground
  marble island to background view. A single coffee cup steams on
  counter (hint of inhabitation).
- 9–11 s: glides up suspended staircase (rising 8 ft in 2 s with
  Steadicam smooth lift). Reveals upper hallway.

[CLIMAX 11–13.5 s]
Camera enters primary bedroom: wide view, bed centred, full-wall window
opens to ocean. Light floods room. Camera pauses at threshold, holds
still for 1.5 s.

[RESOLUTION 13.5–15 s]
Slow camera dolly back through bedroom doorway. Pull-back reveals
hallway, then re-frames the panoramic view through the bedroom window.
Property name + listing tag appears bottom-frame at 14 s. Holds. Fade
to black.

[LIGHTING] Golden hour: warm 3200 K directional light streaming
through windows. Volumetric god rays in entry. Soft fill ambient. No
practicals (turn lamps off — natural light sells the property).

[CAMERA] Steadicam throughout (gimbal-smooth, no handheld jitter).
24–35 mm wide for room reveals; 50 mm for detail moments. f/4 deep
focus to keep architecture sharp throughout.

[GRADE] Warm nostalgia, restrained: 3200 K, slight saturation boost on
warm tones, +5% lift on shadows. Skin / wood / stone tones priority.

[AUDIO] Sea breeze + distant gulls ambient throughout at −6 dB. No
foley except subtle curtain rustle. Cinematic-emotional score arrives
at 2 s very softly, builds slowly, peaks at 11 s on bedroom reveal.
Resolves to held quiet on final beat. No VO (let property speak).

[OUTPUT] 15 s, 16:9, 720p.
```

## Worked example — Apartment listing, 10 s

```
[HOOK 0–2 s]
Macro on a brass door handle. At 0.5 s key turns; door clicks. At 1 s
door swings open; camera passes through threshold into bright apartment.

[ACTION 2–7 s]
Continuous Steadicam flow at 2 ft/s through living room: morning sun
through windows, soft overcast lighting outside, plants on sill,
furnished space.
- 2.5 s: camera turns left into kitchen — pan + dolly combined; bright
  marble + white cabinets.
- 4 s: continues through to bedroom door; pauses on threshold; reveals
  bed with morning light.
- 5–7 s: enters bedroom, slow micro-orbit to show full space.

[CLIMAX 7–10 s]
Camera reaches large window at far wall; rack focus from foreground
plants to view beyond — city skyline, soft morning haze. Window light
overexposes briefly (dreamy bloom). Address / asking-price appears
lower-third at 8.5 s. Camera holds.

[LIGHTING] Soft overcast outdoor + warm interior practical balance.
Naturally diffused, no harsh shadows.

[CAMERA] Steadicam, mostly continuous flow. 24 mm wide for spatial
reveals. f/4 deep focus.

[GRADE] Clean, slightly warm, true colour priority. Not stylised.

[AUDIO] City morning ambient (distant traffic, soft urban). Subtle
foley: door click on hook. Light, mid-tempo strings score from 2 s.

[OUTPUT] 10 s, 16:9, 720p.
```

## Common pitfalls

- **Too many cuts.** Real estate sells through *flow*. A 15 s clip with 8
  cuts feels like an MLS slideshow.
- **Handheld camera.** Reads as iPhone walkthrough, not premium listing.
  Always Steadicam-smooth.
- **Cool / sterile lighting.** Cool light kills warmth. Always lean warm
  (3000–3500 K) or natural (golden hour, soft overcast).
- **Empty without purpose.** An unfurnished space needs strong light and
  movement to feel inviting; otherwise it reads as abandoned.
- **Front-only angles.** Walk *through* spaces; don't just point at them.
- **Heavy stylisation.** Bleach bypass / cyberpunk / heavy grain reads
  wrong for property. Clean grade only.
