# Social Hook — Higgsfield Seedance 2.0

For platform-native short-form video where the hook *is* the entire
strategy. TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts. The brief is always the
same: stop the scroll in 0.5 s, hold for 2 s, deliver payoff by 8 s.

## When to use

- "Viral video", "scroll-stopping", "TikTok / Reels / Shorts."
- Any short-form content where conversion = completion rate.
- When the user asks for "engagement" / "views" without a more specific
  vertical brief.

For genre-led short-form (cinematic, anime, e-commerce) → use the matching
genre skill but mind the social-platform constraints (9:16, 8 s, mobile
framing).

## What's unique to social hook

1. **9:16 vertical, always.** The clip is consumed in a phone scroll feed.
2. **Centre-frame composition.** Top and bottom 15% are eaten by platform
   UI (caption, like buttons, profile widget). Don't put critical content
   there.
3. **First 0.5 s is everything.** Algorithm bias rewards videos that hold
   attention past the half-second autoplay-preview threshold.
4. **Captions matter.** Most viewers watch with sound off initially.
   Front-load text overlay or visual story that works silent.
5. **Pattern interrupt > beauty.** A weird, anomalous, "wait what" frame
   beats a gorgeous static one.
6. **Loop-friendly endings.** Last frame = first frame design lets the
   algorithm autoplay-loop, doubling watch time per impression.

## Social-specific hooks

All 12 generic hooks from [shared/hook-framework.md](../../shared/hook-framework.md)
apply with extra emphasis on:

| Hook                       | Phrasing |
| -------------------------- | -------- |
| **Caption + visual hook**  | "Frame 1: bold caption text 'Wait until 0:05' + intriguing visual. Hooks AND signposts payoff." |
| **Anomaly in plain scene** | "Mundane scene (kitchen, street) with one impossible element (object floating, wrong colour, glitch). Viewer leans in." |
| **Question on screen**     | "Frame 1 type: 'Have you ever seen ___?' Visual answers question over next 6 s." |
| **Loop tease**             | "First and last frame are visually identical (same composition). Last frame's audio cliffhangs into first frame's audio." |
| **Fast-cut info dump**     | "Six 1-second cuts in 6 s, each delivering one fact / image / beat. Viewer can't predict next cut." |

## Worked example — Anomaly hook, 8 s

```
[HOOK 0–2 s]
Mundane kitchen counter. Coffee being poured into a mug — but the coffee
flows *upward* out of the mug, defying gravity. Camera locked at 50 mm
equivalent.

Caption overlay (top third, mobile-safe area): bold sans-serif type,
"this took us 40 takes." Caption appears with subtle bounce at 0.3 s.

[ACTION 2–7 s]
Camera slowly pushes in over 5 s. Coffee continues to flow up; pools in
mid-air above the mug, forming a floating brown sphere. At 4 s a hand
enters frame and gently pokes the sphere — it ripples like water but
stays cohesive.

At 5.5 s sphere splits into two smaller spheres orbiting each other.

At 7 s spheres collapse back into mug with a satisfying glug.

[CLIMAX 7–8 s]
Mug is now full. Hand picks it up; first sip. Cut to title / brand if
applicable. (Or end on a loop-friendly frame matching opening.)

[STYLE] Photoreal kitchen, single anomaly. Slight magic-realism feel.

[CAMERA] Locked frame, slow push-in. f/2.8.

[GRADE] Warm domestic, slight teal-orange. Cosy.

[AUDIO] Kitchen ambient. Subtle whoosh on coffee defying gravity at
0.3 s. Soft musical motif from 1.5 s, builds to 5.5 s, resolves on
satisfying glug at 7 s.

[OUTPUT] 8 s, 9:16, 720p.
```

## Worked example — Caption-led question hook, 8 s

```
[HOOK 0–2 s]
Bold caption fills mobile-safe upper third: "What if your phone could
SMELL?" White text on a soft-blur background of a phone in someone's
hand. Caption types in letter-by-letter over 0–1 s.

At 1.5 s caption pulses; phone screen lights up.

[ACTION 2–6 s]
Macro on phone screen — UI shows a "scent profile" interface, animated
smell-graph visualisation (cherry blossom, cedar, citrus icons appear
in sequence with delicate ease-in animations).

At 4 s: hand brings phone toward a coffee cup; phone "detects" coffee —
UI updates to show coffee-aroma graph.

At 5.5 s: hand brings phone toward a flower; UI updates to floral
profile.

[CLIMAX 6–8 s]
Wide shot: caption "(yes, this is real, link in bio)" at the bottom-
mobile-safe area. Phone screen shows brand logo. Cuts to brand identity
or fades to black on a beat.

[STYLE] Clean tech-product feel. Soft pastel palette. UI is bold + minimal.

[CAMERA] Macro pushes + cuts between phone and environment. Mostly
locked, occasional handheld for "real life" shots.

[AUDIO] Bright synth bell on caption letter-typing. Light electronic
score from 2 s. Subtle UI sound on each scent-detect at 4 / 5.5 s.

[OUTPUT] 8 s, 9:16, 720p.
```

## Common pitfalls

- **16:9 in a vertical platform.** Letterboxed video reads as recycled
  TV ad. Always 9:16.
- **Critical content in mobile UI safe-zone.** Top 15% = caption space;
  bottom 15% = like / share buttons. Plan composition around these.
- **Slow first second.** A pretty pan that doesn't pay off in 0.5 s loses
  the auto-skip viewer.
- **Hook with no payoff.** Promising "wait until the end" without
  delivering tanks watch-through and trust.
- **Ignoring loop potential.** A clip that ends abruptly performs worse
  than one whose final frame matches the first.
