# Timeline Structures

Seedance 2.0 outputs are 4–15 seconds. The structure below maps narrative
beats onto duration. Pick the closest length and adapt.

## 4 s — compact moment

```
0.0–1.5  Hook         (extreme: black→burst, reverse motion, audio drop)
1.5–3.0  Single beat  (one action, sustained)
3.0–4.0  Punctuation  (climax hold or hard cut to black)
```

**Beat pattern:** Impact → Build → Release.

**Use when:** social hooks, product reveals, single-line punchlines.

## 8 s — viral standard

```
0–2      Hook          (from hook-framework.md)
2–4.5    Establish     (location, character, mood)
4.5–7    Escalate      (tension or motion increases)
7–8      Climax        (peak moment, optional freeze)
```

**Beat pattern:** Attention → Context → Tension → Release.

**Pacing:** slow in the first 3 s (viewer acclimation), fastest 4–6 s
(engagement peak), settling 6–8 s (resolution).

**Use when:** TikTok / Reels organic content, e-commerce ads, brand teasers.

## 10 s — premium short

```
0–2      Hook
2–3.5    Establish     (location / character / mood)
3.5–6    First action  (introduces conflict or interest)
6–8.5    Escalate      (raises stakes, faster pace)
8.5–10   Climax        (resolution, emotional landing)
```

**Beat pattern:** Grab → Ground → Build → Peak → Resolve.

**Audio alignment:**
- 0–2 s: ambient or sudden hook sound
- 2–3.5: main melody enters
- 3.5–6: drums / percussion layer
- 6–8.5: music swell
- 8.5–10: peak or fade

**Use when:** YouTube Shorts, premium brand content, music video segments.

## 15 s — full narrative arc

```
0–2       Hook                    (must grab in 2 s)
2–4.5     Establish               (set time, place, mood)
4.5–7     Subject introduction    (who/what matters)
7–10      Rising action           (conflict, tension)
10–12.5   Climax                  (peak intensity)
12.5–15   Resolution / denouement (emotional landing, final image)
```

**Beat pattern:** Hook → Setup → Subject → Rise → Peak → Land.

**Velocity blueprint:**
- 0–4.5 s: slow, deliberate (~2 ft/s camera). Don't overwhelm.
- 4.5–10 s: medium-fast (~3–4 ft/s). Active engagement.
- 10–12.5 s: very fast or locked (5+ ft/s, or static). Climax intensity.
- 12.5–15 s: slow, contemplative (~1–2 ft/s). Resolution space.

**Music alignment:**
- 0–2: hook sound (silence + drop, or stinger)
- 2–4.5: theme establishes
- 4.5–7: melody develops
- 7–10: drums / bass / orchestration layer
- 10–12.5: crescendo, climax sync
- 12.5–15: resolution / fade

**Use when:** full short narratives, cinematic vignettes, brand stories,
luxury real estate, fashion campaigns, music videos.

## Choosing length

| Goal                                  | Length |
| ------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Single hook, single payoff            | 4 s    |
| Hook + payoff with context            | 8 s    |
| Mini-narrative with rising action     | 10 s   |
| Full arc with character + resolution  | 15 s   |

If in doubt, **start at 8 s**. It's the most forgiving length — long enough
for context, short enough to maintain attention without needing a second
climax.

## Common timing mistakes

- **Putting the hook at second 3.** Too late. The hook *must* land by 2.0.
- **Holding establishing too long.** 4 s of "look at this nice room" before
  any action loses the viewer.
- **No climax beat.** A 15 s clip that just runs at uniform velocity feels
  longer than one with a deliberate peak.
- **Under-specified pacing.** "Cinematic, 10 s, samurai walks" gives Seedance
  no rhythmic guidance. Specify what happens *when*.
